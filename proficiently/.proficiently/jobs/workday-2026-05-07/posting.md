**Company:** Workday

## About the Role

The position focuses on leading customer engagements with AI technology expertise. The successful candidate will ensure enterprise customers maximize value from Workday's AI products throughout their partnership lifecycle, managing deployments and driving long-term adoption.

## Key Responsibilities

- End-to-end responsibility for several customers, starting with product deployment through partnership completion
- Retention management and identification of expansion opportunities
- Timeline, risk, and cross-functional coordination for large-scale deployments
- Strategic guidance on AI transformation initiatives
- Change management implementation and end-user education
- Concurrent project leadership for multiple implementations
- Executive steering committee presentations and quarterly business reviews

## Required Qualifications

- Demonstrated experience leading medium to large enterprise implementations or transformation initiatives
- 3+ years of experience in either enterprise application implementation (Workday, Oracle, NetSuite, SAP, etc.) or strategy consulting roles

## Desired Competencies

- Problem-solving ability with unstructured challenges
- Rapid learning across business domains
- Persuasive communication skills
- Complex client management capability
- Executive presence and collaborative approach
