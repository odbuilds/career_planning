## Role Overview
RemotePass seeks a Product Manager to lead AI integration across their platform. Not a typical PM role — focuses on implementing AI meaningfully within complex workflows around global payroll, compliance, and payments.

## Key Responsibilities
- Establish and execute the AI product strategy across the platform
- Collaborate with engineering teams to build, validate, and refine solutions rapidly
- Investigate user challenges in operational domains and transform unclear requirements into actionable decisions
- Balance competing priorities between implementation speed, precision, and user confidence
- Progress products from concept through validation to growth phases
- Serve as a connector between technical capabilities and practical customer needs

## Requirements
- Experience developing and launching products in uncertain, rapidly-changing contexts
- Comfort navigating AI and data-intensive product spaces (ML expertise not required)
- Focus on addressing genuine user problems rather than feature-driven development
- Ability to operate across strategic planning and tactical execution
- Willingness to work with undefined parameters and establish clarity

## Preferred Qualifications
- Background in fintech, HRTech, or similarly intricate operational sectors
- Prior exposure to large language models, workflow automation, or production AI systems
