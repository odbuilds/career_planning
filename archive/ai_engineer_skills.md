# AI Engineer Skills: POC to Production

## Key Obstacles
Reliability gaps, lack of evaluation, poor observability, cost surprises, data/compliance issues, no prompt CI/CD, and weak product judgment.

## Skills (Research-Validated, March 2026)

| Skill | Most In Need (1-5) | Rarest (1-5) |
|---|---|---|
| **RAG architecture** — chunking, re-ranking, hybrid search | 5 | 3 |
| **Multi-agent architecture** — orchestration, context management, failure handling | 4 | 5 |
| **Evaluation & evals design** — LLM-as-judge, regression harnesses | 3 | 4 |
| **Observability for LLMs** — tracing, logging, monitoring | 3 | 4 |
| **Prompt engineering at scale** — versioning, CI/CD, systematic testing | 3 | 3 |
| **Product sense** — knowing when not to use AI, scoping "good" | 3 | 5 |
| **Compliance & data governance** — GDPR/HIPAA in LLM pipelines | 3 | 5 |

## Key Takeaways

- **RAG** is the only skill companies explicitly headline in job ads (13.6% of postings)
- **Multi-agent** has the biggest near-term gap — 94% of engineering leaders report shortages; demand:supply >3:1
- **Product sense, compliance, and multi-agent** are the rarest — require judgment and experience that can't be learned from tutorials
- **Evals and observability** are becoming table stakes but aren't yet primary hiring requirements
- **Prompt engineering** at a basic level is commoditised; only production-grade CI/CD practices remain scarce
