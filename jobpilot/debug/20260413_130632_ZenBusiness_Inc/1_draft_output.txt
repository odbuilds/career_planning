Dear Hiring Manager

Improving a voice bot's F1 score from under 50% to 96% required moving past simple intent recognition to a deep analysis of 2 million customer utterances. This level of systematic optimization is what I intend to bring to Velo as your Conversational AI & Prompt Engineer.

I recently built a LinkedIn Post Agent using LangGraph that treats prompt engineering as a continuous deployment cycle rather than a one-time task. I used HumanFirst to manage model routing and variable injection, ensuring that every prompt was validated against sample sets before being integrated into the pipeline. By implementing a dedicated judge optimizer using GPT-4o, the system uses a reflection loop to catch inconsistent outputs and rewrite content before it ever reaches a user.

My work on vid2doc further addressed the challenge of making non-deterministic systems behave reliably in production. I designed a multi-step pipeline that uses Whisper for transcription and Claude to reason about stable UI states for screenshot extraction. To prevent the system from delivering low-quality documentation, I implemented a conditional regeneration pass triggered by an LLM quality score, ensuring the output met a specific threshold before human review.

I have spent much of my career debugging the gap between a well-structured prompt and a reliable production interaction. I once watched a high-volume voice bot consistently fail on simple inputs like zip codes, which taught me that agentic reliability is won or lost in the edge cases. I find the challenge of building these guardrails—ensuring an agent remains helpful without hallucinating—to be the most critical puzzle in conversational design.

I bring a practical understanding of how to shape agentic behavior through deliberate constraint and automated evaluation. I want to apply this to ZenBusiness to help founders navigate complex compliance and business tasks through seamless, trustworthy AI interactions.

Oliver Day
